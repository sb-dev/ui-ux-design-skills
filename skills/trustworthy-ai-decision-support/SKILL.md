# trustworthy-ai-decision-support

Apply this pack when an AI system assists a consequential human decision and the interface must preserve source distinction, uncertainty, user authority and correction.

## Production profile

Prioritise:

- verified source evidence versus AI inference;
- calibrated uncertainty communication;
- practical human authority over final action;
- correction, disagreement and override;
- automation-bias resistance;
- explanation proportional to the decision;
- auditability where required;
- preservation of original source material.

## Core skill deltas

### `uiux-research`

Consider trust calibration, automation bias and accountability as candidate mechanisms where evidence supports them. Do not treat AI-risk terminology as proof of the product problem.

### `uiux-design`

Generate materially different authority and correction models when uncertainty remains. Keep source facts and AI inference distinguishable in the interaction model.

### `uiux-evaluate`

Evaluate provenance, comprehension, uncertainty, reliance, correction and user control. Reject interfaces where human override is nominal rather than practical.

### `uiux-handoff`

Specify source/inference semantics, uncertainty behaviour, correction/override, action authority and audit behaviour where material.

## Must remain stable

Core evidence provenance, accessibility, approval, fidelity selection, preservation, diagnosis and bounded-refinement rules remain authoritative.

## Precedence

Explicit project/user instructions and approved UX decisions outrank this pack.

## Boundaries

This pack does not implement AI models, explainability engines, regulatory policy or production decision systems.
