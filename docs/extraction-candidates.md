# Cross-Domain Extraction Candidates

**Status:** Observed after Stage 14 review  
**Date:** 7 September 2026

This file records concepts that may later qualify as Production Skills family abstractions. It does not promote them automatically.

## Already family-owned

- cheapest adequate representation;
- deliberate commitment;
- preservation of approved work;
- correction of the smallest responsible unit;
- production intelligence above execution;
- skill vs command distinction;
- layered evaluation;
- no universal quality score;
- Extension Pack semantics;
- escaped defect → regression fixture.

## Promote for family research

### Progressive example ladder

Observed independently in Narrative, Music, Video and UI/UX.

Candidate principle:

> A Production Skills repository should provide examples that progress from a small isolated capability proof to representative end-to-end production.

Do not standardise exact level names or counts.

### Advertised example ↔ benchmark linkage

Observed independently in Narrative, Music, Video and UI/UX.

Candidate principle:

> Public showcase examples used as evidence of claimed production capability should participate in benchmark coverage.

Do not require every documentation snippet to become a benchmark.

## Observe, do not promote yet

- dedicated preservation-test surface;
- stable artifact identity / lineage contract.

## Keep UI/UX-specific for now

- evidence → human problem → mechanism → interaction requirement → hypothesis → validation traceability;
- evidential confidence and burden;
- human-mechanism model;
- UX fidelity dimensions;
- interaction requirements and human-performance requirements.

Revisit only after another domain independently demonstrates substantially equivalent operational semantics.
